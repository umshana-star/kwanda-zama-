## 2. Full Store Description (Google Play)

Transform your customer support and appointment booking operations with **Zama AI** — the intelligent, autonomous WhatsApp business concierge and salon appointment hub.

Designed for modern business owners, salons, and boutique service providers, Zama AI bridges your customer inquiries with automated intelligence, seamless calendar booking, and biometric-grade security.

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 🤖 Autonomous WhatsApp Agent Service
- Ingest customer inquiries directly through WhatsApp Business webhooks or direct messaging.
- Automatically verify service pricing, check availability, answer common questions, and book salon appointments.
- Intelligent human handoff escalation when complex edge cases arise.

#### 🌙 Customizable 'Away' Responder
- Enable Away Mode when outside business hours or during high-volume sessions.
- Automatically dispatch courteous, customized auto-replies with immediate logging.

#### 🎙️ Integrated Voice-to-Text Dictation
- Dictate quick updates, replies, and notes on the go with zero friction using speech recognition.

#### 🔐 Biometric & Salted PIN Vault Security
- Protect sensitive client conversation logs, financial booking records, and API credentials.
- Dual-layer protection: Android Hardware-Backed KeyStore Biometrics (Fingerprint/Face) with salted PIN fallback.

#### 📦 Offline Persistence & Complete Data Portability
- Local Room SQLite database maintains fast access to complete interaction logs.
- Search historical client chats by keyword, sender, or AI reasoning trace.
- Export full conversation logs to JSON anytime with Android system share integration.

#### ⚡ Minimalist & Futuristic UI Themes
- Switch seamlessly between Futuristic Neon Glow and Minimalist Dark interface modes.
